FILMBENCH
Crop Wiggle

Tools for working with film scans. Everything runs in your browser, so your photos are never uploaded.

  • Crop Frame a scan to a cinema ratio and export at its original resolution and format. → 1 scan → 1.85 · 2.35 · 2.6 · 2.77 · custom
  • Wiggle Align frames from a Nimslo, Nishika, Reto 3D or other multi-lens camera into a steady wigglegram. → 2–8 frames → GIF · 1080 · 1600 · original

Face detection runs on your device and is only used to align frames. Built with face-api.js and gif.js (MIT). Source on GitHub.
